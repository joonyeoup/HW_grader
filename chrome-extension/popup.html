How to Use

  1. Configure your OpenAI API key above
  2. Go to Gradescope grading interface
  3. Select the question you want to grade
  4. Click the "AI Grade All" button that appears
  5. Watch as it grades all students automatically!
📄 Multi-page papers: When the submission shows "page X of Y" with Y > 1 (e.g. a research paper or report), the extension automatically navigates every page and sends the full document to the AI for holistic rubric grading. Rubric items beyond the 0–9 keyboard range are clicked directly.
